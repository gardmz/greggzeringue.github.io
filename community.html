GIVING BACK

Giving back to the community has always been a core value for me. Throughout my career, I’ve had the privilege of volunteering with a wide range of charities and organizations, including Jewish Career and Family Services, the Atlanta Community Food Bank, Share Our Strength, the Atlanta Chapter of the Cystic Fibrosis Foundation, the New Orleans Food Bank, Second Harvest Food Bank, Sandals Caribbean Charities, and the Women’s Home of SWLA. In addition to this work, I’m proud to serve on the board of our local culinary school, where I help support the next generation of hospitality professionals and give back to the industry that has given me so much.
