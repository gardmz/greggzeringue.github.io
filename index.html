Gregg Zeringue

As an Executive Chef for large casino and island resorts, I have learned that its best to stay true to the location you are cooking in and ensure the dishes reflect the local flavor but always give the guest a bit of excitement from something new. I try to incorporate a little bit of my southern New Orleans background as much as I can. It is a unique cuisine that is admired and often duplicated around the world. I grew up in the “Sportsman’s Paradise” and learned early on to work with fresh seafood and have always enjoyed working seafood into dishes. My preference is always to use local ingredients and support the local economy whenever possible. I have learned in my experience that diversity in food options and experiences really speak to our guests. At Beaches we were four different properties in one, we had all options for all guests. Now at L’auberge I get to oversee front of the house management as well as a very large and diverse culinary team.

My Inspiration

A huge part of the inspiration in my cooking and lifestyle comes from growing up in one the greatest cities in the world, New Orleans. A city that needs no introduction. My entire life I have been inspired by the culture of this city and it has greatly impacted my cooking.

My Philosophy

I believe that as a leader I am responsible for my teams performance. The better I prepare them with the tools, education and experience the more successful we will all be.

My Motivation

My motivation comes from so many aspects of my life and personality. My family plays a huge part in what drives me to succeed. I find that I thrive when given added responsibilities and am given the opportunity to learn and grow outside my comfort zone. My goal at the end of my career is to be remembered as not just a great leader but as an innovator in my field.

My Destination

Working in the Caribbean greatly influenced my career. I really enjoyed the people and the culture. I wasn't aware how much I would miss it until I left. Being able to expand my career and hold such a respected position with a great company would truly be life altering.
